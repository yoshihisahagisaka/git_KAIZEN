# atLIB Corporate Philosophy / Slogan — Brand Language v1

Status: **CANONICAL — CORPORATE BRAND LANGUAGE**  
Date: 2026-09-13

## 1. Corporate Philosophy — DECIDED

> **ITで、企業に自由を。人々に未来をつくる。**

This is atLIB's corporate philosophy.

Do not silently rewrite, shorten, paraphrase, or replace it in official customer-facing materials unless a separate creative copy is explicitly labeled as such.

---

## 2. Corporate Slogan — DECIDED

> **ITを企業成長の武器にする**

This is atLIB's corporate slogan.

Do not replace it with newly generated slogans such as「ITで、会社の未来をともに創る」or「ITで、会社の未来をもっと前へ。」when the material is intended to display atLIB's official slogan.

---

## 3. Relationship to IT経営KAIZEN

IT経営KAIZEN should connect naturally to the corporate philosophy and slogan.

Corporate Philosophy:
> **ITで、企業に自由を。人々に未来をつくる。**

Corporate Slogan:
> **ITを企業成長の武器にする**

> **経営者が実現したい会社のFUTUREから逆算し、ITを使って企業を継続的に良くしていくコンサルティング事業。**

The relationship is:

```text
Corporate Philosophy
ITで、企業に自由を。人々に未来をつくる。
        ↓
Corporate Slogan
ITを企業成長の武器にする
        ↓
IT経営KAIZEN
FUTUREから逆算し、FACTで決め、ITで企業を継続的に良くする
```

---

## 4. Customer-facing Material Rule

When atLIB corporate brand language is displayed:

- Philosophy must use the exact decided wording.
- Slogan must use the exact decided wording.
- Service-specific headline/copy may be created separately, but must not be presented as the corporate philosophy or slogan.
- Visual assets for IT経営KAIZEN should not invent a replacement corporate slogan merely for layout convenience.
