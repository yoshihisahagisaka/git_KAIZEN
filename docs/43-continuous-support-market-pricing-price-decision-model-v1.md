## 2. Market Interpretation

### FACT from observed public offers
1. Low-price market exists around ¥30k–¥100k/month for limited advice/help desk/light operations.
2. ¥120k–¥250k/month is common among publicly priced limited-hour or limited-device operational services in the sampled evidence.
3. A major vendor currently publicly offers a 40h/month comprehensive/professional tier at ¥576k/month.
4. Advisor/consulting and operational execution are often priced separately or by hours.
5. Several competitors expose hours/device counts directly as the commercial unit.

### What this does NOT prove
- It does not prove ¥650k is too expensive.
- It does not prove ¥1m is unsellable.
- It does not prove competitor service quality/equivalence.
- It does not establish WTP for IT経営KAIZEN.

## 3. atLIB Difference to Protect

IT経営KAIZEN 継続支援 should not compete as another low-price help desk/time bank.

Current service design includes:
- Assessment-based Future / FACT / KGI starting point
- Accepted Work Scope
- operational execution
- FACTACT operating platform
- Work → FACT / Evidence / Current State
- Monthly KAIZEN
- Quarterly Management Review
- Annual KGI / CHANGE Review
- Human Decision / Authority
- existing-vendor connection and Commercial Neutrality

Core commercial distinction:
> **時間を売るのではなく、経営と決めたCHANGEへ向けてIT Workを継続運用し、その結果をFACTで確認・KAIZENするService Capabilityを売る。**

However, differentiation is not permission to ignore market alternatives. Customer must perceive the additional value.

## 5. Three Price Scenarios

### Scenario A — Market Entry
Purpose: reduce first-contract friction while learning actual delivery FACT.

PROPOSAL:
- S: ¥350k/month
- M: ¥500k/month
- L: ¥800k/month
- Custom: quote

Approximate direct contribution using current cost hypothesis:
- S: ¥150k / 42.9%
- M: ¥167k / 33.4%
- L: ¥300k / 37.5%

Risk:
- M margin buffer appears thin before FACTACT/product/sales/overhead.
- low initial price may create difficult future repricing.

### Scenario B — Balanced
Purpose: maintain meaningful delivery margin while staying relatively close to upper public managed-operations benchmarks.

PROPOSAL:
- S: ¥400k/month
- M: ¥600k/month
- L: ¥900k/month
- Custom: quote

Approximate direct contribution:
- S: ¥200k / 50.0%
- M: ¥267k / 44.5%
- L: ¥400k / 44.4%

Interpretation:
- M ¥600k is close to the publicly observed OPTiM Pro ¥576k/40h, while atLIB M hypothesis represents ~50h internal load plus KAIZEN/management cycle.
- This does not prove equivalence, but gives a useful market anchor.

### Scenario C — Premium Managed KAIZEN
Purpose: price the management/KAIZEN layer strongly and target customers with higher complexity/value.

PROPOSAL:
- S: ¥450k/month
- M: ¥650k/month
- L: ¥1,000k/month
- Custom: quote

Approximate direct contribution:
- S: ¥250k / 55.6%
- M: ¥317k / 48.8%
- L: ¥500k / 50.0%

Risk:
- M/L require clear differentiation from time-based outsourcing.
- L ¥1m likely requires customer complexity/business impact beyond ordinary 200-person help desk perception.

## 7. Recommendation — PROPOSAL

For initial product launch, **Scenario B — Balanced** is the strongest current candidate.

Reasoning:
1. Scenario A M gives insufficient apparent buffer under current cost assumptions.
2. Scenario C can be justified only after stronger customer-value proof and/or higher-complexity targeting.
3. Scenario B M ¥600k sits near an observed current public benchmark of ¥576k for a 40h professional outsourcing tier, while atLIB intends to include operational Work plus management/KAIZEN cycle.
4. S ¥400k avoids entering the commodity help-desk price zone and keeps the product positioned as Managed IT KAIZEN.
5. L ¥900k retains room for broader Scope without immediately crossing ¥1m psychological threshold.

This is a **PROPOSAL**, not a price decision.

## 8. Important Commercial Change — Do Not Publish S/M/L First

Even if internal Capacity Bands are retained, customer-facing pricing should not necessarily lead with an S/M/L table.

PROPOSAL:
> **継続支援 月額40万円〜（Accepted Work Scope / Service Capacityにより個別設計）**

Why:
- avoids plan-shopping by price
- preserves Assessment → Scope → Capacity logic
- avoids customers interpreting S/M/L as feature tiers
- allows early FACT collection before thresholds are stable

Internal quoting can continue to use S/M/L/Custom as capacity controls.

If this approach is adopted, Sales Sheet shows starting price only; Quote / Scope Sheet shows actual monthly price and assumptions.

## 9. Assessment Price Relationship

Current canonical Assessment price remains:
> **¥1,200,000 pre-tax**

At Balanced M ¥600k/month, Assessment equals two months of recurring fee. This is not inherently problematic because Assessment is a distinct Decision/Baseline service, not an onboarding fee.

Do not discount Assessment automatically because recurring support follows.

Potential future commercial experiments such as crediting part of Assessment fee are NOT decided and should not be introduced without evidence.

## 10. Initial Transition Fee — PROPOSAL

Current Scope Sheet identified transition work:
- scope confirmation
- environment confirmation
- Authority / approver mapping
- vendor/escalation mapping
- intake setup
- procedures/Knowledge connection
- open Work handoff
- KGI/Baseline connection

This creates real delivery cost.

Two possible models:
A. separate Initial Transition Fee
B. include within first-month / minimum-term economics

Do not decide amount until transition Human Work is estimated.

Recommendation: design transition cost next before final monthly price decision.

## 11. Target Customer by Capacity — PROPOSAL

### S

Example: Service Desk with lower volume or one defined Workflow family.

### M
Primary initial target candidate.
Example: 100–250 employee SME, one-person/small IT team, Service Desk-centered Scope with management/KAIZEN cycle.

### Custom
Large/complex environment, expanded hours, exceptional responsibility, regulated requirements, high incident/event load.

## 12. Sales Positioning Against Market

Do not say competitors are inferior.

Useful distinction:

Typical market comparison axis:
> **何時間 / 何件 / 何台を対応するか**

IT経営KAIZEN axis:
> **1年後に何を変えるか → そのために何を継続運用するか → 日々のWorkからFACTをつくる → 毎月KAIZENする → 本当にCHANGEしたか確認する**

> **「何時間使えるか」ではなく、「会社のITをどう変えるか」から設計します。**

## 13. Price Validation FACT to Collect

For first 3–5 pilot/early customers, record:
- quoted monthly price
- Accepted Scope
- expected/actual Work Volume
- actual Human Work
- transition Human Work
- SLO attainment
- specialist escalation
- reporting/review Human Work
- customer objections
- lost-deal reason
- alternative/competitor price when customer voluntarily provides it
- willingness to expand/reduce Scope
- renewal/continuation
- verified CHANGE/customer value

Do not collect competitor confidential information improperly.

---

## 14. Price Review Rule

After sufficient early-customer FACT:
> **Quote Assumption → Actual Delivery FACT → Customer Value / Sales FACT → Difference → Human Decision → Price / Capacity Model Update**

Do not change price because one month had many tickets.
Do not preserve price merely because it was initially published.

## 15. Current Decision Gate

Before final Price DECISION, remaining major UNKNOWN:
1. transition Human Work / cost
2. actual fully loaded delivery cost
3. FACTACT monthly infrastructure/product allocation
4. company target gross margin / contribution requirement
5. actual sales acceptance

The first three can be estimated internally before launch. Sales acceptance requires market testing.
